dotMate

Ask about periods and menstrual health in your own words, typos included, or type calendar to estimate your next period.

dotmate, a C++20 program running here as WebAssembly

This is the real C++ program, compiled to WebAssembly. It runs entirely in your browser and nothing you type is sent anywhere. If you choose to be remembered, only your name and a scrambled copy of it are kept, in this browser's local storage.

Answers come from a fixed set of reviewed, sourced entries rather than a chatbot, so it can't make things up, but it also can't answer everything. For education only, not medical advice. If something worries you, talk to a doctor.
